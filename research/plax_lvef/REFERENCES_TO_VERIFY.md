before generating BibTeX; abstracts do not prove all implementation details.

1. Gao,Z.;Yurk,D.;Abu-Mostafa,Y.S. *Machine Learning with Scarce Data:
   Ejection Fraction Prediction Using PLAX View*.
   [PMLR publisher record](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v301/gao26a.html).
   Publisher record lists2026;OpenReview material labels MIDL2025.
   Resolve version/year explicitly rather than mixing citation metadata.
   This is the closest PLAX regression/data-labeling reference. Its MAE6.86
   is on a different evaluation cohort and cannot establish our superiority.
2. Tran et al. *A Closer Look at Spatiotemporal Convolutions for Action Recognition*.
   [CVPR2018 paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_cvpr_2018/papers/Tran_A_Closer_Look_CVPR_2018_paper.pdf).
   Attribute the factorized R(2+1)D architecture,not its invention to us.
3. Lin,J.;Gan,C.;Han,S. *TSM:Temporal Shift Module for Efficient Video Understanding*.
   [ICCV2019 publisher page](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_ICCV_2019/html/Lin_TSM_Temporal_Shift_Module_for_Efficient_Video_Understanding_ICCV_2019_paper.html).
   Attribute temporal shifting;its reported device timings are not ours.
4. Howard et al. *Searching for MobileNetV3*.
   [ICCV2019 paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_ICCV_2019/papers/Howard_Searching_for_MobileNetV3_ICCV_2019_paper.pdf).
   Attribute the frame backbone;our cine temporal head and evaluation are separate.
5. Ozkan et al. *M(otion)-mode Based Prediction of Ejection Fraction using Echocardiograms*.
   [Author preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.03759).

   anatomical EF evidence. Confirm original view/cohort before comparing.

Add verified distillation,echocardiography standards,MIMIC-IV-ECHO dataset and
statistical-reporting references from primary sources. Never turn a candidate
reference into an invented DOI,paper result,clinical guideline or Q1 claim.
